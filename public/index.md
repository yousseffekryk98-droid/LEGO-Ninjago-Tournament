# Ninja Tournament — Open-Source Fan Remake

> Unofficial clean-room 3D browser fan remake inspired by the discontinued 2015 LEGO Ninjago Tournament mobile arena game.

- Play: https://lego-ninjago-tournament.pages.dev/
- About / FAQ: https://lego-ninjago-tournament.pages.dev/about.html
- Source: https://github.com/yousseffekryk98-droid/LEGO-Ninjago-Tournament
- License: MIT for original project code and original project materials
- Maintainer: Youssef Fekry

## Current features

- 51 playable fighters
- elemental combat and fighter-themed kick effects
- Spinjitzu
- Tornado of Creation team ultimate in Free Play
- permanent fighter upgrades using studs
- half-heart and full-heart health pickups
- Tournament, Challenge and Dojo modes
- Fighters, Gallery, loadout and control systems
- keyboard, gamepad and mobile touch controls
- PWA support and a Capacitor Android packaging path

## Technology

TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, Playwright and Capacitor.

## Status and affiliation

The project is playable and under active development. It is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LEGO, TT Games, Hellbent Games or other rights holders.
