# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code rather than
people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, and
targeted hostility are not acceptable.

## Collaboration

- Give actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while still addressing problems clearly.
- Credit contributors and sources.
- Respect copyright, licenses, and community rules.
- Do not pressure contributors to share private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and community
participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated abuse may
result in a ban from project spaces.

For moderation or conduct concerns, use a private GitHub security/advisory channel
when appropriate rather than posting sensitive personal information publicly.
